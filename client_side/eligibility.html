About This Study

We are inviting undergraduate computer science students who are familiar with Java to take part in a research study on how programmers understand code.

What you'll do: one in-person session of up to 90 minutes (including the consent process) in a room in the GITC building at NJIT. Using your own laptop, you'll read up to 20 short Java methods on a study website and work out what each one returns for a given input.

What you'll receive: food after the session, and a chance to win a $50 Amazon gift card in a drawing held after all sessions.

Privacy: your email is used only to check eligibility, schedule your session and notify the gift-card winner. It is never linked to your study responses.
