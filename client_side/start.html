Code Comprehension Study

Welcome, and thank you for taking part.

  • You will first answer two short surveys about your background and programming experience.
  • Next, you will work on one practice snippet to get used to the interface.
  • Then you will see a series of short Java methods, one at a time. For each one, you will work out what value the method returns for a given input, and tell us whether you have seen the code before.
  • You cannot go back to earlier snippets.
  • The study pauses automatically for short breaks, with a longer break halfway through.
  • A timer at the top of the page counts up. Pause it before you stop working (for example, to ask a question or take an unscheduled break) and resume it when you continue.
  • Scratch paper and a pen are allowed. Please do not use any other tools or websites.
  • Need help? Pause the study and raise your hand.
